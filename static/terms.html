Terms & Conditions

Last updated: 2026

1. Bookings

Seats are held for 10 minutes while you complete payment. Unpaid holds expire automatically and the seats return to availability. A booking is confirmed only after successful payment verification.

2. Ticket entry

Arrive at least 15 minutes before showtime. Present your booking reference (e.g. CB1A2B3C4) at the counter or gate. Late entry is subject to venue policy.

3. Snack pre-orders

Pre-ordered popcorn, drinks and combos are prepared for pickup at the snack counter on your booking date. All food items are vegetarian. Prices include taxes.

4. Payments

All online payments are processed via Razorpay. By paying you agree to Razorpay's terms. Failed payments do not charge your method; the seat hold stays active until it expires so you can retry.

5. Cancellations

Unpaid holds can be cancelled by you at any time from My Tickets. Paid bookings follow the venue's refund and cancellation policy displayed at the counter.

6. Conduct

Venue rules, the film's certificate (U/A/A) and applicable law apply. The management reserves the right of admission.

7. Account

Login uses a one-time password sent to your email. You are responsible for keeping that code private during the 5-minute validity window.
