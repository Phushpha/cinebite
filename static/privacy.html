Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026

What we collect

Your email address (for OTP login and booking confirmations), your bookings, and the snacks you order. Payment details are handled entirely by Razorpay — card numbers, UPI IDs and bank credentials never touch our servers.

How we use it

Only to create your account, confirm your bookings, and show your ticket history. We do not sell or share your data with third parties other than Razorpay (payment processing) and our email OTP delivery provider.

Cookies & local storage

We store your login session and cart in your browser's local storage so you stay logged in. Clearing browser data signs you out.

Retention

OTP codes expire in 5 minutes and are deleted. Booking records are retained for order history. You may request deletion of your account data at any time by contacting the venue.

Payments

Payments are processed by Razorpay Software Pvt. Ltd. under its own privacy policy. We store only the payment ID, order ID and booking reference.
